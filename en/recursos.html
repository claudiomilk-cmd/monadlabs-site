Everything MONAD does

One app to talk, pay and get things done — private from the very first message.

💬

Messages and groups

Text, voice notes, photos, videos and files, all end-to-end encrypted. Groups, status updates and scheduled messages.

📞

Voice and video calls

Direct device-to-device connection whenever the network allows, also encrypted.

💎

MNDA wallet

Send and receive MNDA, the network's currency, with a QR code.

🛵

Deliveries

Shops, customers and couriers in one place. Payment is held in escrow and released only when the order arrives.

🚗

Rides

Request or offer rides with a live map and two-way ratings.

🎫

Events and 🎙️ podcasts

Create events, sell tickets, record episodes and follow your favorite channels.

🤖

AURA, the assistant

Answers questions about the app and helps with everyday tasks.

🧰

Tools

Pomodoro, notes, habits, calendar, clocks, scanner and more — no extra apps needed.

🔧

Car diagnostics

With an inexpensive Wi-Fi OBD-II adapter, see RPM, temperature and fault codes explained in plain language.
