Why MONAD

Real privacy

End-to-end encryption: each message is encrypted on your phone and can only be opened on the recipient's device. In transit — and on the server — it is unreadable.

No tracking

No ads, no data sales. Creating an account takes no phone number, email or ID.

Built to resist blocking

If one internet route is blocked, the app automatically finds another — including through the Tor network.

Everything in one place

Chats, groups, calls, wallet, deliveries, rides, events, podcasts and everyday tools.

Who MONAD is for

📞

For people living far from family

Call and send photos and voice notes to the people back home at no cost. When someone speaks another language, the built-in chat translator helps.

🔒

For people who take privacy seriously

No phone number, and end-to-end encryption. Our Security page shows what already protects you — and what is still missing.

🛵

For couriers and neighborhood shops

Orders, customer chat and payment in one app. The money is held in escrow and released when the delivery arrives.

🌐

For people facing internet blocks

When one route goes down, the app finds another on its own, including the Tor network. We don't promise miracles — we promise to try every path.

🧩

For people who build apps

Build and test mini-apps in the Developer Studio and publish them on the MONAD App Store, which runs inside the app itself.

🌱

For people just getting started

One step at a time, with AURA to answer questions. And one simple rule: no one from MONAD will ever ask for your recovery words.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Download the app from the download page (Android or PC).
  2. Create your wallet — it is your account. Write your recovery words on paper and keep them somewhere safe.
  3. Invite someone by showing your QR code or sending your address.
