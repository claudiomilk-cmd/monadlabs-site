Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. No subscription and no ads.

Do I need a phone number, email or ID?

No. Your account is your wallet, created on your own device.

What if I lose my phone?

Install MONAD on the new device and enter the recovery words you wrote down. Without them, nobody — not even the MONAD team — can recover the account.

What is MNDA?

It is the currency used inside the network to pay for services between people (deliveries, rides, tickets). It is not an investment and we make no promise that it will gain value.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

So it doesn't depend on a company that could take the app down. You download it directly from here, and every file carries a digital signature you can verify.

Android warns the app is from an "unknown source". Is it dangerous?

That is the standard warning for any app installed outside the Play Store. Download only from this page and, if you like, check the fingerprint (SHA-256) shown on the download page.

Does it work on iPhone or Windows?

On Windows, yes — get it on the Download page. Not on iPhone yet. Today: Android, Windows and Linux PCs.

Does it work where the internet is censored?

The app automatically looks for alternative routes, including the Tor network. It can't be guaranteed everywhere, but it was designed for exactly that.
